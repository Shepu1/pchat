2. 🧩 What this service is

Pchat shepu (SHEPU ROOMS) is a web application you run in your browser. It is a folder of HTML, CSS and JavaScript — static files, not a hosted chat service with a central message server.

Rooms are created and stored through your own Google account. A saved room writes its chat history to a Google Sheet in the room owner's Google Drive and answers messages through the owner's own Apps Script deployment. A quick room has no backend at all: messages live in the participants' browsers and travel over the public relay described in section 6.

If somebody else hosts this copy of the app, that host is the operator of the software; the room owner is the operator of each room's data. Neither the software's authors nor the host can read a room's history unless they also own the room's Google Sheet.

3. 📝 Your content and rooms

You keep ownership of what you send. Messages, images, files, nicknames and rooms you create remain yours. The app does not claim any rights over them, and there is no central database in which to store them anyway.

You may use the app for personal and small-group chat. You are responsible for the content you post and for the rooms you create — including making sure you have the right to share what you put in them.

The room owner controls the room's saved history: they can open the backing Google Sheet, export it, or delete it. Deleting the room and its Sheet permanently removes that chat history for everyone.

4. 🔑 Room access and links

Anyone who has a room link can open that room unless the owner has protected it with a room password. Treat invite links like keys: share them only with people you want inside, and regenerate the link from the Owner panel if it leaks.

Room owners can remove or ban members, lock a room to make it read-only, and delete it. Invites to rooms sent through Friends are encrypted on your device, so only that friend can open them — but the plain room link itself is still a key.

8. 👋 Ending your use

You can stop using the app at any time and without giving a reason. To leave cleanly:

  • Revoke Google access from your Google account permissions page.
