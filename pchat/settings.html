Privacy Model

Where is my data stored?

Room data is stored in the room owner's connected Google storage rather than in a central Pchat shepu message database. Each room's chat history lives in a Google Sheet that the owner controls.

Who can access my room?

Only people with the room link can join. Guests communicate through the room's private Apps Script endpoint — they never receive direct edit access to your Sheet.

What does Pchat shepu store?

Pchat shepu stores room ID → deployment URL mappings in your browser's localStorage, plus a local offline cache of room messages in IndexedDB. No message content is stored on external third-party servers.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the room from the Owner Panel. Optionally, also delete the Google Sheet from your Google Drive. This permanently removes all chat history.
